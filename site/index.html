ENGINEERING TRANSPARENCY

Simulating a car is not the same as validating one.

This is a seven-coordinate coupled ride model: body heave, body pitch, body roll and four independent wheel vertical displacements, each with velocity. A separate longitudinal / yaw / engine / thermal model supplies driving and steering inputs. The 3D suspension geometry is an explanatory linkage visualization, not a solved rigid-body multibody constraint system.

Road inputs, tire normal contact clipping, suspension parameters, and vehicle dimensions are illustrative. The engine, tyres, braking and thermal components remain reduced-order. True suspension engineering requires measured hardpoints, nonlinear kinematics and compliance, bushings, bump stops, geometry, load transfer calibration, contact and external test data. Flow-native units and equation tests are tracked in the source repository.
